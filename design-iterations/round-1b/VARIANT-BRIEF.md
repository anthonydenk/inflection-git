# Shared brief for all Round-1b variants — Inflection Capital Management

You are building ONE full homepage variant as a single self-contained HTML file (all CSS/JS inline; Google Fonts allowed; no external images — all art is CSS/SVG placeholder in your assigned lens, to be replaced by real photography in Phase 3). Desktop-first but responsive at 390px.

## Non-negotiable: the story and the brand

**This is a San Francisco firm and the page must tell the story of them.** Place is the narrative spine: the city, the bay, the fog and Pacific light, the bridges, Silicon Valley wealth creation. A visitor should feel "this firm is OF San Francisco" — not told it, shown it.

**Brand identity is locked to inflectioncm.com:**
- Palette: deep evergreen (#0B2E20 family) as the anchor; pale mint/seafoam (#D6E7DD family) as the light tone; white and soft sky tones. One palette, no departures. Accent work is done with mint-on-evergreen and evergreen-on-mint.
- Type: elegant serif display for the wordmark and headlines (Freight-like — use "Source Serif 4", "Newsreader", or "Lora"; high-polish, not Playfair-flashy), clean quiet sans for nav/labels/body support.
- Signature moves from the live site you may build on: soft curved section transitions (rounded sweeps between light and dark sections), full-bleed atmospheric photography temperature, generous calm pacing, the serif "Inflection" wordmark.
- Voice: patient stewardship, quiet authority. "We serve as trusted stewards for generations to come."

**Inspiration whitelist:** inflectioncm.com and the Phase-0 research sites only (ICONIQ's gallery quiet, Jordan Park's texture-as-brand and italic serif promise, Bessemer/Rockefeller trust conventions, Compound/Forge modern private-markets clarity). The development-branch "memorandum" design is explicitly OFF-LIMITS as influence: no chapter TOCs, no dot leaders, no ledger-document framing, no "document artifact" conceits.

## Guardrails

Never: colors outside the brand palette; purple/blue gradients; Inter/Roboto as voice; three-equal-cards defaults; centered-everything monotone rhythm; 3D blobs; stock-cliché compositions (no generic mountain-summit-man); marketing-speak ("unlock", "empower", "seamlessly", "elevate"); emoji; exclamation marks; pop-in-all-at-once; scroll-jacking; removed focus rings; gradient text.
Always: commit HARD to your lens; real hover/focus-visible states; staggered once-only IntersectionObserver reveals honoring prefers-reduced-motion; section rhythm with a plot (density then air, dark then light); clamp() type scale; institutional trust ($100M-family credible); straight apostrophes.
