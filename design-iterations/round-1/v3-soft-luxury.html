Who We Are

I.

Inflection Capital Management is a partner-owned and operated multi-family office based in Silicon Valley, dedicated to working with clients to preserve and grow their wealth and legacy.

Our careers have been dedicated to working with wealth creators, families navigating periods of transition, and family offices, including their foundations.
